## How the models decide [Proposed]
1. Each cycle, every model may submit theses in the three-sentence form from SOUL.md §2.
2. Combined, they may add at most 3 new theses per cycle (the cap is from AGENT.md §3).
3. A thesis enters the shared book only if it passes the QUANT, RISK and HERMES gates (from AGENT.md). RISK can only reject.
4. Every proposal, approval and rejection is logged in DECISIONS.md and attributed to the model that made it.

## Cadence
- Three cycles per US trading day, at 10:30, 12:30 and 14:30 ET (the schedule 2.0 ran). [Proposed]
- Each cycle follows the AGENT.md §8 sequence, and hashes are committed before execution. [AGENT.md]
